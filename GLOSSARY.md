## Authority and Governance

**Absolute constraints** — Law, executed contracts, security, safety, and
client confidentiality. Imposed by `MASTER.md` §7 and binding on every tier,
including `MASTER.md` itself.

**Tier** — How binding a document is. Tier 1 (`MASTER.md`) through Tier 8
(temporary artifacts). See `governance/AUTHORITY.md` §4.

**Scope** — The subject a document is entitled to govern. A document outside
its scope has no authority regardless of tier.

**Source of truth** — The single document authoritative for a subject.
Recorded in `governance/DOCUMENT_REGISTRY.md`.

**Downstream impact pass** — Checking and correcting documents that depend on
one you changed. Required for substantive changes.

**Defective** — A document containing a statement that contradicts a
higher-tier document. Defective documents are corrected, not reinterpreted.

## Status Vocabulary

**Confirmed** — A founder-approved decision. Binding. Agents may rely on it as
fact.

**Provisional** — A working direction, not finally approved. Usable if
labelled as provisional.

**Open** — Registered in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. **Not known.** Must never be
filled with an invented value.

**Deprecated** — Superseded, retained for history. Never relied upon.

## Commercial

**Boutique strategic digital systems partner** — The company's confirmed
commercial identity (D-001). Not an agency, not a website vendor, not an AI
company.

**Primary problem** — Fragmented, outgrown, or underperforming digital
infrastructure. The single problem presented externally (D-003).

**Commercial progression** — Entry (Strategic Website & Digital Foundation) →
Expansion (Digital Systems & Workflow Improvement) → Recurring (Optimization,
Support & Managed Digital Systems). See D-005.

**Entry offer** — Strategic Website & Digital Foundation. The primary way
clients begin. **Not** an audit or strategy session — those are sales
instruments (`BUSINESS.md` §9).

**Narrow problem, broad capability** — Present one problem externally; retain
full capability internally (D-004).

**Established SMB** — The confirmed client category (D-006): businesses that
have proven they can sell and operate, with capacity to invest. Not yet
quantified (`ASSUMPTIONS.md` A-005).

**Qualified** — An opportunity meeting `ICP.md` fit criteria, assessed under
`sops/sales/QUALIFICATION.md`. Not merely "interested."

## Delivery

**Delivery lifecycle** — The ten phases in `DELIVERY.md` §2. The single
definition; `MASTER.md` no longer holds a competing one.

**Quality gate** — A checkpoint where phase outputs must be acceptable before
proceeding (`DELIVERY.md` §6).

**Material change** — A change to objectives, deliverables, integrations,
timeline, or cost. Requires a change order (`sops/delivery/SCOPE_CHANGE.md`).

**Acceptance criteria** — Specific verifiable conditions that end an
engagement. QA verifies against these.

**Definition of done** — `MASTER.md` §9.2. Requirements satisfied, deliverable
exists, verification occurred, limitations disclosed, documentation updated.

## AI and Automation

**Agent** — A specialized operating role. Grants capability, not authority
(`MASTER.md` §10.1).

**Autonomy level** — 0 Manual · 1 AI-assisted · 2 Human-approved ·
3 Automated with monitoring · 4 Autonomous within boundaries
(`AUTOMATION.md` §3). Above Level 2 requires founder authorization.

**Work product** — What an agent produces. Analysis, evidence, recommendation,
or draft — never automatically truth.

**Silent failure** — An automation producing incorrect results without
signalling. The failure mode `AUTOMATION.md` §7 exists to prevent.

**Fabrication** — Supplying a value the founder has not decided. The most
damaging failure mode in this system (`RISKS.md` R-006).

## Measurement

**Qualified traffic / leads** — Visitors or leads matching `ICP.md`, not
volume.

**Measured vs. estimated** — Every reported figure must be labelled as one or
the other (`METRICS.md` §8).

**Bottleneck** — The primary constraint on business output. Secondary systems
are not optimized while it remains (`MASTER.md` §12.3).
