## 1. Purpose
Generate awareness and qualified demand while building evidence for The Milbourne Group's positioning.

## 2. Marketing Objective

Attract the right prospects by communicating relevant problems, valuable
outcomes, credible expertise, and a clear next step.

### 2.1 Positioning Constraints

These are binding on every marketing artifact (D-001 – D-004, D-007, D-009):

- **Lead with the client problem**, not with capability or technology.
  The confirmed problem is fragmented, outgrown, or underperforming digital
  infrastructure.
- **Never lead with AI.** AI and automation are operating advantages, not the
  value proposition. AI-led messaging places the company in the commodity AI
  category it has decided not to compete in (`RISKS.md` R-002).
- **Never compete on price.** The position is boutique / premium specialist.
- **Present the narrow problem, not the full capability menu.** Breadth is
  internal (D-004); `SERVICES.md` §3 is not a marketing menu.
- **National reach is not indiscriminate marketing.** The geographic model is
  national and remote-first (D-008), but initial acquisition stays focused and
  evidence-driven.
- **No invented facts.** No unearned metrics, client names, case-study
  results, guarantees, or prices. See `BRAND.md` §6 and `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

## 3. Core Channels
Channels may include:
- Company website
- SEO
- Case studies
- Educational content
- Direct outreach support
- Partnerships
- Referrals
- Social distribution where strategically justified

Do not treat every channel as mandatory. Initial channel selection is
registered as open (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q-008) and depends on industry focus
(Q-001) and founder advantages (Q-005). Until decided, concentrate effort
rather than dispersing it across all candidates.

## 4. Content Strategy
Create content around:
- Client problems
- Decision frameworks
- Common digital mistakes
- Before/after improvements
- Process transparency
- Technical and strategic insights
- Evidence from completed work

Content must not expose confidential client information.

## 5. Website Role
The company website should:
- Explain positioning
- Clarify offers
- Build trust
- Demonstrate capability
- Capture qualified demand
- Provide an appropriate CTA

## 6. SEO
Prioritize useful, intent-aligned content and technically sound implementation. Avoid manipulative or low-value content production.

## 7. Marketing Metrics
Measure:
- Qualified traffic
- Qualified leads
- Conversion rate
- Channel contribution
- Cost where measurable
- Pipeline influence

Detailed definitions belong in `METRICS.md`.

## 8. Experimentation
Document hypothesis, change, expected outcome, measurement method, and result. Do not confuse increased activity with improved marketing.
