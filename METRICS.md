## 1. Purpose
Measure outcomes that support decisions.

## 2. Metric Rule
Every tracked metric should answer:
- What is measured?
- Why does it matter?
- How is it calculated?
- Where does data come from?
- How often is it reviewed?
- Who acts on it?

## 3. Business Metrics
Track when data is available:
- Revenue
- Recurring revenue
- Average project value
- Gross contribution/margin
- Net margin where practical
- Revenue concentration

## 4. Sales Metrics
- Qualified opportunities
- Discovery-to-proposal rate
- Proposal-to-close rate
- Sales cycle duration

## 5. Marketing Metrics
- Qualified traffic
- Qualified leads
- Conversion rate
- Channel contribution

## 6. Delivery Metrics
- Delivery duration
- Rework
- Defects
- Client satisfaction where measured
- Project profitability

## 7. Automation Metrics
- Estimated time saved
- Failure rate
- Recovery time
- Automation coverage
- Cost

## 8. Metric Integrity
Do not optimize a metric that does not reflect meaningful value. Record assumptions and known data limitations.

## 9. Review
Review metrics on a cadence appropriate to decision frequency. Change a metric definition only with documentation to preserve interpretability.
