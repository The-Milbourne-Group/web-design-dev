## 1. Purpose
Sequence capability building so the company does not attempt to build every system simultaneously.

## 2. Phase 1 — Foundation
Complete and maintain:
- Business knowledge architecture
- Brand foundation
- Offer definitions
- ICP definition
- Sales process
- Delivery process
- Technical governance
- Automation governance
- Security governance
- Metrics

## 3. Phase 2 — Commercial Readiness
Build:
- Company website
- Portfolio/evidence system
- Proposal system
- Discovery system
- CRM/pipeline process
- Initial acquisition channels

## 4. Phase 3 — Delivery Readiness
Build:
- Client onboarding
- Project templates
- QA checklists
- Launch procedures
- Client reporting
- Reusable design/development assets

## 5. Phase 4 — Automation
Automate stable processes with monitoring:
- Research assistance
- Documentation workflows
- Reporting
- Repetitive QA
- Internal operations
- Approved sales support

## 6. Phase 5 — Optimization
Use measured evidence to improve:
- Positioning
- Offers
- Conversion
- Delivery margin
- Reusability
- Automation reliability

## 7. Roadmap Rule
Do not mark work complete because a document or automation exists. Completion requires operational usefulness and appropriate verification.

## 8. Sequencing Dependency

Phase ordering assumes an answer to revenue urgency and founder capacity
(`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q-006). A revenue-urgent configuration would move Phase 2
acquisition work ahead of Phase 1 completion; a system-building configuration
would not. Do not treat the current order as settled until Q-006 is resolved.

## 9. Current Priority Selection
At any time, identify:
- Primary objective
- Primary bottleneck
- Highest-leverage next action

Avoid simultaneous strategic priorities unless capacity genuinely supports them.
