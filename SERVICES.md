## 1. Purpose

Define the offers, what each is intended to solve, and how scope is
controlled so that engagements remain deliverable and profitable.

## 2. Commercial Progression

Services are organised by the confirmed progression (D-005), not as a flat
catalogue. Every engagement should be locatable in one of these three stages.

```
ENTRY                    EXPANSION                      RECURRING
Strategic Website   →    Digital Systems &         →    Optimization, Support
& Digital Foundation     Workflow Improvement           & Managed Digital Systems
```

### 2.1 Entry — Strategic Website & Digital Foundation

**The primary way clients begin.** An end-to-end engagement establishing a
digital foundation that supports credibility, conversion, and growth.

Typically combines discovery, strategy, information architecture, content
planning, design, development, QA, and launch — sequenced per `DELIVERY.md`
and built to `WEB_STANDARDS.md`.

Solves: a digital presence that fails to establish credibility, communicate
value, or generate qualified opportunities.

*Exact deliverables, inclusions, exclusions, and timelines are open — Q-011.*

### 2.2 Expansion — Digital Systems & Workflow Improvement

Engaged after the foundation is in place and value has been demonstrated.

Covers digital systems, integrations, workflow improvement, and automation
implementation where processes are stable and sufficiently defined.

Solves: fragmented customer journeys, disconnected systems, and manual
processes creating operational friction.

### 2.3 Recurring — Optimization, Support & Managed Digital Systems

The durable relationship stage: post-launch maintenance, measurement,
conversion improvement, SEO support, performance work, and automation
expansion.

Solves: digital infrastructure decaying or failing to keep pace with growth.

## 3. Underlying Capabilities

The company retains broad capability internally (D-004). These are the
components assembled into the offers above — they are **not** a separate menu
to sell from, and marketing must not present them as the primary offer set.

| Capability | Applied in |
|---|---|
| Website strategy — discovery, audience analysis, IA, conversion strategy | Entry |
| Website design — usable, accessible, conversion-conscious interfaces | Entry |
| Website development — reliable implementation of approved strategy and design | Entry |
| Web applications — where a problem genuinely requires application functionality beyond a marketing site | Expansion |
| Automation systems — workflow analysis and implementation for stable, defined processes | Expansion |
| Integrations — connecting disconnected business systems | Expansion |
| Optimization and support — analytics, CRO, SEO support, maintenance | Recurring |

**Sales instruments.** Audits, assessments, and strategy sessions establish
understanding and qualify fit. They are not the entry offer (`BUSINESS.md`
§9) and must not create unlimited unpaid consulting.

## 4. Offer Definition Standard

Before any offer is presented commercially, it must define:

- Problem
- Ideal client
- Desired outcome
- Included scope
- Exclusions
- Deliverables
- Dependencies
- Client responsibilities
- Acceptance criteria
- Timeline assumptions
- Pricing model
- Expansion opportunities

An offer missing any of these is not ready to sell.

## 5. Scope Discipline

Do not accept vague terms such as "anything needed" or "unlimited revisions"
without explicit commercial controls.

A material change in objectives, deliverables, integrations, or requirements
requires a change order before implementation. Policy: `DELIVERY.md` §5.
Procedure: `sops/delivery/SCOPE_CHANGE.md`.

## 6. Service Selection

Recommend the smallest engagement capable of solving the problem reliably.

Do not upsell unnecessary complexity. Recommending an expansion engagement
before the entry foundation is sound is a scope failure, not a sale.

## 7. Unresolved Configuration

Package contents, deliverables, turnaround times, formal guarantees, and all
pricing are registered in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` (Q-007, Q-011).

Until the founder configures them, they must not be stated in a proposal,
on the website, or in any agent output. Quote "to be determined" rather than
an invented figure.
