## 1. Client Context
Demonstrate understanding of their business before proposing anything. Draw
from discovery notes.

## 2. Problem
The problem in the client's own words, and its business consequence.

## 3. Desired Outcome
What success looks like to them.

## 4. Recommended Approach
The approach and **why** — the reasoning is the differentiator
(`BUSINESS.md` §7). Recommend the smallest engagement that solves the problem
reliably (`SERVICES.md` §6).

**Engagement stage:** Entry / Expansion / Recurring (`SERVICES.md` §2)

## 5. Scope
Specific and bounded.

## 6. Deliverables
What the client receives. Each must be verifiable.

## 7. Exclusions
What is **not** included. State explicitly — never imply.

## 8. Dependencies and Client Responsibilities
Content, access, approvals, and decisions the client must supply, with timing.
Most timeline failures originate here.

## 9. Timeline Assumptions
Sequence and duration, with the assumptions it depends on. Label as
assumptions, not commitments.

## 10. Commercial Terms
**Pricing:** To be determined *(Q-007 — do not fill without founder input)*
**Payment schedule:** To be determined
**What triggers a change order:** `sops/delivery/SCOPE_CHANGE.md`

## 11. Acceptance and Change Process
How work is accepted, and how changes are handled.

## 12. Assumptions
Everything this proposal depends on being true.

## 13. Next Step
One clear action.
