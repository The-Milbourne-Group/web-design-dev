# DECISION REGISTER

**System:** Operations / Governance
**Purpose:** Single permanent record of significant decisions, their reasoning, status, and consequences.
**Authority:** Tier 2 for entries with status `Confirmed` — founder-approved decisions binding on Tier 3 and below. Subordinate to `MASTER.md`.
**Parent Authority:** `MASTER.md` (Tier 1 — supreme), `governance/AUTHORITY.md`
**Applies to:** All company-level decisions. Project decisions belong in the project's own decision log.
**Owner:** Founder
**Status:** Active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-29
**Review trigger:** Any significant decision; Discovery Round 2 completion.
**Related:** `STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.md`, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, `sops/operations/DECISION_LOG.md`, `templates/strategy/DECISION_BRIEF.md`

---

## How to Use This Register

Log a decision here when it is **significant**: it changes strategy, commits
money or capacity, creates an obligation, sets a policy, changes an authority
boundary, or would be expensive to reverse.

Do not log routine execution choices. Those belong in project documents.

Procedure: `sops/operations/DECISION_LOG.md`. Template for the underlying
analysis: `templates/strategy/DECISION_BRIEF.md`.

**Statuses:** `Confirmed` (founder-approved, binding) · `Provisional`
(working direction, medium confidence) · `Superseded` (replaced; retained
for history) · `Reversed`.

**ID format:** `D-###`, assigned sequentially and never reused.

## Strategic Decisions

### D-001 — Adopt boutique strategic digital systems partner positioning
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** The Milbourne Group operates as a boutique strategic digital
systems partner helping established businesses strengthen the digital
infrastructure that supports credibility, conversion, operational efficiency,
and growth.

**Context.** The company holds broad capability across websites, applications,
systems, and automation. Presenting that breadth to the market produced no
differentiated commercial position.

**Alternatives considered.** Commodity website provider; generic AI agency;
lowest-cost provider; high-volume standardized vendor. All rejected as
undifferentiated or margin-destructive.

**Reasoning.** Narrow the externally presented problem while retaining broad
internal capability. Compete on business understanding and measurable
outcomes rather than price or novelty.

**Consequences.** Governs `BUSINESS.md`, `BRAND.md`, `SERVICES.md`,
`MARKETING.md`, `SALES.md`. Marketing must not lead with technology.

**Source:** `STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.md`

### D-002 — AI is an operating capability, not the market-facing identity
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** AI and automation are internal operating advantages. The
customer-facing value proposition centres on credibility, conversion,
efficiency, systems improvement, and growth. The company does not position as
an AI-first or AI-native business to the market.

**Context.** `MASTER.md` §2.1 described an "AI-first ... AI-native digital
business" and `BUSINESS.md` §2 described an "AI-first digital services
company." Both directly contradicted the founder-approved competitive
position, which excludes "generic AI agency" and "AI novelty" differentiation.

**Alternatives considered.** Retain AI-first identity and revise the strategic
configuration instead. Rejected: the strategic configuration is founder-approved
and its Risk 2 explicitly requires AI positioning to remain subordinate to
client value.

**Reasoning.** Buyers purchase business outcomes. AI-led positioning invites
comparison with commodity AI vendors, which is the exact category the company
has decided not to compete in.

**Consequences.** `MASTER.md` §2 and `BUSINESS.md` §2 were corrected on
2026-08-29. Internal AI leverage remains fully endorsed and is governed by
`AUTOMATION.md`. Any future marketing or agent output leading with AI as the
primary differentiator is defective.

**Source:** `STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.md`; `exports/EXECUTIVE_STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.docx` §6, §10 Risk 2

## System Governance Decisions

### D-012 — Single authority model replaces four competing precedence statements
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** `MASTER.md` is the ultimate authority in the system. Beneath it:
founder-approved strategy, then domain sources of truth, then SOPs, agents,
templates, project knowledge, and temporary artifacts. Authority is scoped as
well as tiered. `governance/AUTHORITY.md` holds the detailed precedence model
by delegation from `MASTER.md` §5.

The absolute constraints — law, executed contracts, security, safety, client
confidentiality — bind every tier because `MASTER.md` §7 imposes them, not
because they rank above the constitution. This keeps `MASTER.md` supreme
without making it citable as a licence to override law or a signed agreement.

**Context.** The repository contained four incompatible precedence
statements, including a self-contradiction inside `MASTER.md` — §1.1 declared
it "the root operating constitution" while §5.1 ranked it below domain
documents. Any decision could be justified by selecting a precedence rule.

**Reasoning.** The system cannot resolve conflicts without one ranking. The
constitution/domain split preserves the legitimate intent behind the old
§5.1: specific documents govern their own subject.

**Consequences.** `MASTER.md` §5.1 rewritten. Both READMEs replaced. The
client-requirements tier was corrected: client authority is scoped to the
engagement and never overrides the §7 constraints.

### D-013 — Markdown is the source of truth; `.docx` is a generated export
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** `STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.md` is authoritative.
`exports/EXECUTIVE_STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.docx` is a presentation export
carrying no independent authority.

**Context.** The `.docx` was ranked as an authority but is binary,
non-diffable, and unreadable to agents — while containing *more* content than
the markdown that outranked it (decision register, risk analysis, Discovery
Round 2). The richer document ranked lower and was invisible to the system.

**Consequences.** All unique `.docx` content was ported into
`DECISIONS.md`, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, and `RISKS.md`. Regenerate the export
after changing the markdown; never edit the export as a source.

### D-014 — Confirmed decisions are removed from "configuration required" lists
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** A decision recorded as `Confirmed` here must not appear as an
open configuration item in any other document.

**Context.** `BUSINESS.md` §15 and `ICP.md` §8 listed geographic focus as
requiring founder input after the founder had confirmed national/remote-first
(D-008). Agents would have re-asked settled questions or treated confirmed
strategy as optional.

**Consequences.** `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` is the only register of open items.
Domain documents link to it instead of maintaining private lists.
