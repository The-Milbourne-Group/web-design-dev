**System:** Operations / Governance
**Purpose:** Single permanent record of significant decisions, their reasoning, status, and consequences.
**Authority:** Tier 2 for entries with status `Confirmed` — founder-approved decisions binding on Tier 3 and below. Subordinate to `MASTER.md`.
**Parent Authority:** `MASTER.md` (Tier 1 — supreme), `governance/AUTHORITY.md`
**Applies to:** All company-level decisions. Project decisions belong in the project's own decision log.
**Owner:** Founder
**Status:** Active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-29
**Review trigger:** Any significant decision; Discovery Round 2 completion.
**Related:** `STRATEGIC_CONFIGURATION.md`, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, `sops/operations/DECISION_LOG.md`, `templates/strategy/DECISION_BRIEF.md`

---

## How to Use This Register

Log a decision here when it is **significant**: it changes strategy, commits
money or capacity, creates an obligation, sets a policy, changes an authority
boundary, or would be expensive to reverse.

Do not log routine execution choices. Those belong in project documents.

Procedure: `sops/operations/DECISION_LOG.md`. Template for the underlying
analysis: `templates/strategy/DECISION_BRIEF.md`.

**Statuses:** `Confirmed` (founder-approved, binding) · `Provisional`
(working direction, medium confidence) · `Superseded` (replaced; retained
for history) · `Reversed`.

**ID format:** `D-###`, assigned sequentially and never reused.

## System Governance Decisions

### D-012 — Single authority model replaces four competing precedence statements
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** `MASTER.md` is the ultimate authority in the system. Beneath it:
founder-approved strategy, then domain sources of truth, then SOPs, agents,
templates, project knowledge, and temporary artifacts. Authority is scoped as
well as tiered. `governance/AUTHORITY.md` holds the detailed precedence model
by delegation from `MASTER.md` §5.

The absolute constraints — law, executed contracts, security, safety, client
confidentiality — bind every tier because `MASTER.md` §7 imposes them, not
because they rank above the constitution. This keeps `MASTER.md` supreme
without making it citable as a licence to override law or a signed agreement.

**Context.** The repository contained four incompatible precedence
statements, including a self-contradiction inside `MASTER.md` — §1.1 declared
it "the root operating constitution" while §5.1 ranked it below domain
documents. Any decision could be justified by selecting a precedence rule.

**Reasoning.** The system cannot resolve conflicts without one ranking. The
constitution/domain split preserves the legitimate intent behind the old
§5.1: specific documents govern their own subject.

**Consequences.** `MASTER.md` §5.1 rewritten. Both READMEs replaced. The
client-requirements tier was corrected: client authority is scoped to the
engagement and never overrides the §7 constraints.

### D-015 — Client directories are created at qualification, not onboarding
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** `clients/<client>/` covers the full lifecycle from qualification
through closure, including disqualified opportunities. It is created by
`sops/sales/QUALIFICATION.md`, not by `sops/delivery/ONBOARDING.md`.

**Context.** Integration testing found the sales-to-delivery chain broken: the
qualification SOP produced a written fit assessment, the discovery SOP produced
notes, and neither named a storage location. `clients/` was created only at
onboarding — after qualification, discovery, and proposal had already run. The
placement rule in `governance/KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3 routed prospect
artifacts to "temporary; do not file it," while
`sops/delivery/ONBOARDING.md` §5.2 required carrying assumptions forward from
discovery notes that had nowhere to exist.

Compounding it, `ICP.md` §7 instructed operators to accumulate buyer evidence
to resolve Q-003 — a *blocking* open question — with no location to accumulate
it. The system could not generate the evidence needed to unblock its own
configuration.

**Alternatives considered.** A separate `pipeline/` directory promoted to
`clients/` at agreement. Rejected: it introduces a migration step that can be
skipped, and duplicates the isolation rules that already govern `clients/`.

**Reasoning.** One directory per opportunity, from first qualification,
reusing the existing isolation model unchanged. Retaining disqualified
opportunities is deliberate — that reasoning is the primary evidence for
Q-001 and Q-003.

**Consequences.** Updated `clients/README.md`, `_CLIENT_TEMPLATE/`,
`sops/sales/QUALIFICATION.md`, `sops/sales/DISCOVERY.md`,
`sops/delivery/ONBOARDING.md`, `ICP.md` §7,
`governance/KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `governance/DOCUMENT_REGISTRY.md`,
`CLAUDE.md`, `SYSTEM_MAP.md`.

### D-016 — Requirement traceability is mandatory between discovery and proposal
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** Every requirement entering a proposal must trace to a discovery
finding, an explicit client statement, or a documented assumption. A
requirement with no source is removed, not justified. Procedure:
`sops/sales/SOLUTION_DESIGN.md`.

**Context.** Integration testing found the step between discovery and proposal
entirely undocumented — no SOP, and no agent owning it.
`agents/SALES_AGENT.md` disclaimed solution design as "founder-led

undocumented human knowledge. This is the point where fabricated scope enters
a commercial engagement.

**Reasoning.** The traceability matrix makes fabrication mechanically
detectable: a requirement either has a source or it does not.

**Consequences.** New SOP; `SALES.md` §5 extended with the policy;
`clients/_CLIENT_TEMPLATE/SOLUTION.md` added; sales agent may build the matrix
but not decide the solution.

### D-021 — Company size bands and buyer roles
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** Medium-High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** Small-to-mid-sized established businesses with enough
organizational complexity and purchasing capacity to justify a strategic
engagement.

| Band | Priority |
|---|---|
| 10–100 employees | Primary target |
| 100–250 employees | Secondary, where decision-maker access remains practical |
| 5–10 employees | Selective, where revenue and project economics justify it |
| 250+ employees | Opportunistic until enterprise delivery capacity exists |

**Qualification requirement.** A qualified opportunity should have a meaningful

### D-024 — Balanced revenue and system-building, with a revenue override
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** High · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** Balance revenue generation with deliberate system-building;
revenue takes priority when financial requirements demand it. Avoid both
extremes — building indefinitely before selling, and accepting every project
without developing repeatable operations.

**Operating sequence.** Generate qualified revenue → deliver successfully →
capture reusable knowledge and assets → improve systems from actual delivery
experience → increase standardization as patterns emerge.

**Capacity policy.** Do not commit to more concurrent delivery than the founder
can manage without delayed delivery, declining quality, lost sales, excessive
workload or operational instability.

**Initial capacity assumption.** One major complex engagement at a time, plus
one smaller maintenance, optimization or limited-scope engagement where
capacity allows. Additional capacity comes from repeatable processes,
templates, reusable code, trusted contractors, standardized scopes and better
project management.

**Revenue-first override.** Where immediate revenue requirements become
significant, acquisition and profitable delivery temporarily outrank internal
system development — but reusable delivery knowledge is still captured.

**Resolves.** Q-006.

### D-025 — Value-oriented fixed-scope pricing across three commercial layers
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Status:** Confirmed · **Confidence:** Medium · **Owner:** Founder

**Decision.** Value-oriented fixed-scope project pricing is the primary

roadmap, reduce uncertainty before implementation. **Paid**, and it exists

**Layer 2 — Core implementation.** Website redesign and development, digital

**Minimum engagement rule.** Do not accept a major custom project that cannot

management effort.

**Margin policy.** Target engagements with gross margin sufficient to fund
founder compensation, delivery costs, sales and marketing, software and
infrastructure, taxes and reinvestment. Review pricing against actual delivery
economics, not estimated effort.

**Approved conceptually; not yet policy.** The founder's answer states

becoming policy. **No figure may be quoted on the strength of this decision.**

**Context.** The proposal was the only step in the commercial chain with no

`templates/README.md` was the only entry whose "Used by" named a person rather

1. The sole control preventing a fabricated price or package reaching a

4. `Lost` was a declared terminal status that no procedure assigned, and loss

**Reasoning.** The system could qualify, discover, and design a solution, then
